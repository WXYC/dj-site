Review audio test

This page checks whether this browser can play review audio through a service worker (a small script that fetches the audio a piece at a time): starting, jumping around, and going on playing in the background. You are done when every format has been checked in Safari on the Mac, Safari on an iPhone, and Chrome and Firefox on the Mac, and each browser's copied results are posted on dj-site#2148.

Track links

On the Mac, spike/make-test-bucket.sh presign prints five lines: one per format, and one starting with lock for the lock test's 30-minute track. Paste all five here and press "Save links". Do this once in each browser. Only Safari runs the lock test, so other browsers need just the first four.

To get the lines onto an iPhone, send them to yourself in a private note, paste them here, then delete the note. The links work like passwords: never post them anywhere.

Test steps

Getting the test player ready…

  1. Pick a format

    Do every step below for each format. A ✓ marks a format that is finished in this browser.

  2. Press play

    Listen for a steady tone, with a burst of short high pips every 10 seconds.

    • The worker answered the player's first request
    • The audio is playing
    • The player asked for the next piece by itself (can take half a minute)

  3. Jump around and listen

    Each minute the tone steps up in pitch. Within each minute the pips count up: 1 pip at the start of the minute, 2 pips ten seconds later, and so on up to 6. Press each button, listen, and wait for its line to show a result before the next one.

    Did you hear the right spot?

  4. How far ahead the player loads

    Nothing to do: the page measures this while the audio plays.

  5. Save the lock link first: the fifth line of the presign command, the one that starts with lock.

    1. Turn the sound up: you need to hear it.

    • The worker answered the player while you were away
    • The audio went on past what was loaded when you left

    Did the audio play the whole time?

    Did the audio come back within a few seconds after you dragged the scrubber?

    Did play and pause on the Lock Screen work?

  6. Next format
